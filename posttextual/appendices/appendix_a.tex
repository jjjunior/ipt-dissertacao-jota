\MyParaBold{Questions about suggested solutions}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Would the information generated by the prompt have been useful at the time of the project?
        \item Which of the responses generated coincides with what was implemented?
        \item Is there any information in the generated responses that you disagree with?
        \item Is there information you agree with in the generated responses that still needs to be implemented?
    \end{enumerate}
    
\MyParaBold{Sequence questions}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item What do you think of the proposed sequence?
        \item Does this sequence fulfill the objective of supporting architectural decision-making?
        \item Were any steps unnecessary and could be removed?
        \item Are there any steps that need to be added?
        \item Would you make any changes to the sequence of prompts applied?
    \end{enumerate}
