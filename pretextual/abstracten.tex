\begin{abstracten}
\textbf{Context:} Making design decisions in software architecture is crucial to meeting functional and quality requirements. However, it can be challenging due to uncertainties, tacit knowledge, and integration among various Software Development Life Cycle phases in software construction projects. This research explores integrating generative AI tools, specifically GPT models, to assist architectural software decision-making. For this purpose, we developed new generative AI prompt patterns for application within a sequential approach.
\textbf{Objective:} This study aims to evaluate the outcome of suggestions from the sequence of prompt patterns, using generative AI tools to assist architects in making informed software architecture decisions and understanding their strengths and limitations.
\textbf{Methodology:} The methodology encompasses a comprehensive literature review, a preliminary study, the proposal of five new prompt patterns, the development of a sequential approach to these patterns, and an industry study applying this sequence. The literature review provided valuable insights into the selection of project patterns and revealed a gap in detecting context-sensitive project patterns using generative AIs. The preliminary study focused on using generative AI tools to automatically select project patterns in specific contexts and evaluate their accuracy and potential to guide developers effectively. New prompt patterns, specialized in assisting in architectural decisions, were created, and a sequence of prompt patterns was proposed to evaluate the assistance provided. The industry component of the study involved a project where the software architect assessed the utility of the information generated by the sequence of prompt patterns, utilizing interviews and qualitative analysis.
\textbf{Results:} The preliminary study of a generative AI tool to help developers choose project patterns involved 56 questions from teachers and examiners inserted into ChatGPT, which found that 93\% received accurate responses. This demonstrates the tool’s potential to guide developers in making informed design decisions. The sequence of prompt patterns provides relevant and precise guidance, and the industry studies revealed practical benefits of implementing this approach in real-world contexts, offering valuable insights into the effectiveness, limitations, and perceived utility of AI-assisted decision support in software architecture.
\end{abstracten}
