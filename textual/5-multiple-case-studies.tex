To achieve our research objective, it is vital to assess the prompt patterns sequence to determine their potential uses and whether the suggested patterns are user-friendly. Section~\ref{design-process}  introduced five new versions of prompt patterns, some of which were derived from the patterns used in Section~\ref{patterns-used}, to create a proposed prompt pattern sequence. This chapter presents the outcomes of two case studies that implemented this sequence of prompt patterns, one conducted in a financial institution and the other in a pharmaceutical retail company. We aim to answer the following research question:

\textbf{\textit{RQ3: What are the perceived benefits and challenges of using implementing the proposed sequence of prompts with generative AI tools into the architectural decision-making process from the perspective of software architects?}} -- Considerations: We are interested in gathering software architects' opinions on the reliability of generative AI and whether this approach can facilitate the company's decision-making process.

In each scenario study, we selected a system to apply and evaluate the sequence of prompt patterns. We adopted the multiple case study research method to conduct an in-depth evaluation of the proposed prompt patterns and obtain greater generality about the results. These results are then used to generate a survey and triangulate the evaluation of the sequence of prompt patterns. Such triangulation is relevant as it will increase the degree of reliability of the results~\cite{Easterbrook2008}.

We defined three main steps for the multiple case study(Figure~\ref{keys-steps-multiple-case-studies}). The first consists of obtaining a general understanding of the system's architecture chosen for the study. The next step applies the prompt pattern sequence to guide architectural decisions with information. Finally, we invited the architects who participated in the project decision-making to evaluate and comment on the responses generated by the sequence of prompts.
