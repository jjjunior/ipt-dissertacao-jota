The methodology used in this study is based on case study and retrospective study methods~\cite{desouza2005experiences,runeson2012case}, which have been slightly adapted to meet the specific objectives of our research. Case studies observe and register architectural decisions in real projects and identify specific attributes or relationships~\cite{gustafsson2017single}. Retrospective Studies involve reviewing architectural decisions from completed projects to elicit from the software architect whether generative AI suggestions would have been helpful in their past decisions~\cite{hayes2011impact}. 

The objective is to evaluate whether the Prompt Patterns Sequence developed in this work impacts decision-making processes. We will compare the actual decisions made during the project with the usefulness of the information generated through the Prompt Patterns Sequence.

This adaptation is important to enable a focused analysis of how AI-based hypothetical scenarios could have influenced decision-making processes in previous projects. The combination and minor modifications to established methodologies were not intended to introduce new features but were essential to address the particular nuances of our research question. 

This approach maintains the analytical nature of retrospective analysis and the observational strengths of case studies while providing the necessary structure to explore the specific influence of AI-generated data through the prompt pattern sequence on architectural decision-making.

\MyParaBold{1. Recruitment}
To be eligible for participation in the study, projects in companies had to meet some prerequisites that ensure that the data collected is relevant in detail for a complete analysis:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Recently Completed Project:} Companies must have completed a project recently.     \item \textbf{Significant architectural decisions:} The completed project must involve a major architectural decision to provide insights into the decision-making processes and the impact of these choices on the project outcome.

    \item \textbf{Willingness to share data:} Participating companies must be willing and able to provide detailed and comprehensive data about projects. This includes technical specifications, decision-making processes, results, and the logic behind key architectural choices.

Due to these challenges, a convenience sample was used for the study. They are first asked about their experience working with systems that incorporating software architecture to measure their practical exposure. This is followed by a request to describe their professional trajectory and main experiences in software architecture, which helps in understanding their depth of knowledge and areas of expertise. \MyParaBold{Prompt Patterns Section}

In this interview section, we seek to gain insight into the perspective of architects regarding the architectural recommendations generated by AI using prompt patterns created in this work. We conducted a collaborative process wherein we analyzed the scenario retrospectively. Initially, we asked the participants a fundamental question: whether AI-generated information would have been helpful during the project to evaluate the practical applicability of prompt patterns, which is an essential aspect of our research. Furthermore, the participants were requested to identify the AI-generated responses aligned with what was implemented and discuss any discrepancies or agreements with the AI suggestions.

\MyParaBold{Prompt Patterns Sequence Section}

Following that, the sequence of prompt patterns is assessed. Participants are asked for their thoughts on the proposed sequence and whether it meets the objective of supporting architectural decision-making. They are encouraged not just to critique but also to suggest any steps that could be unnecessary and could be removed or if any steps need to be added to enhance the process. Lastly, they are invited to propose changes to improve the prompt sequence.

By systematically mapping these responses back to the corresponding prompt patterns, we can accurately identify which patterns are most influential in engaging the interviewees and contributing to the perceived usefulness of the suggestions.
